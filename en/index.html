Contact Sheet Support

For macOS 14 or later.

Open Your Photos

Connect a memory card or external drive and select it in the sidebar. To browse a local folder, use the add button beside Local Folders or choose Contact Sheet → Choose Folder.

Organize and Copy

Use the grouping controls in the toolbar to organize photos by time interval or a chosen number of groups. You can also adjust group boundaries manually. Open a group to browse and rate photos, then use the copy action to choose a destination. Wait for the task to finish and check its verification status before disconnecting your device.

Folder Access Problems

Check that the storage device is connected and select the folder again through the app. Allow folder access when macOS asks. Make sure your copy destination is writable and has enough free space.

Missing Photos

Check that the selected folder contains JPEG, HEIF, supported RAW, or video files, then refresh the device. RAW previews depend on the file format and macOS support.

Change Appearance

Open Settings → Appearance to choose light, dark, or system appearance and switch between the native and retro inspectors.

Contact Support

For help or feature requests, email anloetic@foxmail.com.

When reporting an issue, include your app version, macOS version, the steps you took, and any error message. Hide private information in screenshots. You do not need to send original photos, passwords, or full file paths.
