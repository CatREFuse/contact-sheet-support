Privacy Policy

Updated October 24, 2026

Contact Sheet is a Mac app for organizing and copying photos. We do not collect personal information through the app, upload your photos, or use advertising trackers or third-party analytics services.

File Access

The app reads memory cards, external drives, and folders you allow it to access so it can display photos, read capture times, organize groups, and verify copies. Copied files are saved to the destination you choose. At your request, the app can write Finder color tags, export star ratings to XMP sidecar files, or move selected files to the Trash.

Data on Your Mac

Settings, folder permissions, star ratings, group information, copy records, and thumbnail caches are stored on your Mac. The developer does not receive this information. Removing a local folder from the sidebar removes its saved entry and permission without deleting the files in that folder.

Accounts and Permissions

Contact Sheet does not require an account. You can manage the app's file and folder permissions in macOS.

Policy Updates

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and the privacy information on the App Store.
